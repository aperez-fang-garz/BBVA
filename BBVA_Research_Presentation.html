Yes, under a clear
definition of “best”

BBVA is a compelling choice for Spain’s best bank when the assessment prioritises digital leadership, profitability and innovation.

The strongest evidence
Independent app benchmarks, strong reported returns and domestic commercial traction.
The essential qualification
Other banks lead selected measures of satisfaction, efficiency and overall awards.

An evaluative research conclusion, not an unconditional ranking or an investment recommendation.
